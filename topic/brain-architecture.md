---
title: Brain Architecture
---
Brain architecture is the structural organization of a brain runtime and its Markdown knowledge base. It defines stable core components, clear layers, modular boundaries, typed entities, storage conventions, plugins, and explicit extension points that separate core logic from optional capabilities. This modular design supports low coupling, separation of concerns, maintainability, reuse, scalability, and confident evolution as requirements change.

Architecture also provides a framework for evaluating design trade-offs and understanding how structural choices affect contributors, connected systems, developers, agents, usability, and extensibility. Clear documentation of these relationships helps humans and machines understand how the brain operates, navigate its knowledge, and build upon it. A coherent architecture supports technical planning, contributor onboarding, ecosystem development, and informed decisions about future capabilities. Complementary expertise and distributed architectural knowledge further improve resilience and make the system easier to maintain and extend.
