---
title: AI Grant Program
---
AI Grant is a funding initiative founded in 2017 by Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross that supports AI researchers, builders, and open source projects. It provides unrestricted support ranging from $5,000 to $50,000, delivered as cash or compute, allowing recipients to apply resources to their specific needs. The program funds both early-stage experimentation and product-oriented work across areas such as reinforcement learning, model management, drug design, medical imaging, generative art, and privacy tools. AI Grant is distinct from a related accelerator, reflecting multiple support tracks within a broader support ecosystem. Its flexible, general-purpose funding helps maintainers and innovators advance foundational and applied AI projects.
