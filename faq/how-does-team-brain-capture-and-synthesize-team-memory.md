# FAQ

## Answer
Team Brain captures conversations as shared memory and can track Inbox action cards as shared action items. Topic extraction includes both draft and published team material, helping surface recurring themes. The shared Board lets the team work on action items surfaced by conversations.

Sources: [Feature Overview](/docs/feature-overview) and [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).
